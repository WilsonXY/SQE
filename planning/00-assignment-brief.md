# Assignment Brief — Summary

Source: `Assignment/NEW Individual Assignment  Level 3  SQE (100 incourse).doc`

## Learning objectives

1. Describe and critically evaluate a suitable software development methodology for a chosen software development project.
2. Explain the relevant quality assurance processes by explaining the meaning of quality when applied to software.
3. Apply and critically evaluate estimating techniques for time, effort and cost.
4. Create a quality plan and a risk plan for use in project management.
5. Devise, describe, apply and critically evaluate various software metrics.

## Assignment 1 (40%), due Week 7

Required structure, quoted from the brief:

- **Introduction**: introduce the software product, stating the purpose and scope of the topic. Cover the product's background, applications and challenges, defining the product, its importance and the context in which it will be explored.
- **Methodology**: "Select, justify, and critically evaluate the most appropriate software development methodology for the selected software project based on its goals, constraints, risks, and stakeholder expectations."
- **Quality Assurance Process**: "Critically select, evaluate, and apply quality assurance practices that address key software quality attributes, ensuring that the software product aligns with stakeholder requirements and industry standards."
- **Integrating IV&V with the selected methodology**: "analyse and implement the integration of Independent Verification and Validation (IV&V) processes within the [Selected Methodology] framework to facilitate early defect detection, ensure requirements compliance, and improve overall software quality."
- **References**: APA 7th edition.

## Assignment 2 (60%), due Week 14 (for later)

Estimation · Risk Management Plan · Software Metrics · Requirement Analysis (3 common RE problems, causes, impact, best practices) · Conclusion & Recommendation · References.

## Conditions (both assignments)

- A business report with a title page, executive summary, table of contents, list of figures, the report itself, and appendices and illustrations.
- About 5,000 words per assignment.
- **Submitted as a PDF.**
- Citing facts is mandatory and must use credible sources in APA 7th style. No "dumping of data": facts must be made relevant to the project.
- Research topics currently discussed in academic journals and industry, plus the lecture and tutorial topics.
